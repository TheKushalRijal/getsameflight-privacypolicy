SameFlight

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

SameFlight helps people who are on the same flight find each other, chat, and travel together or help one another. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • We never sell your personal information and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • There are no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics in the app.
  • We only connect travellers. We don't take or process payments, and any arrangement between travellers is between them.
  • Your email and phone number are never shown to other users. Your WhatsApp number is only shown to travellers you accept a chat with.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are stored securely in hashed form; we can't see them.

Profile information. Your name, and if you choose to add them, a short bio, the languages you speak, and a profile photo.

Flight information you add. Flight date, flight number, departure and arrival airports, transit stops, departure time, what you're looking for (someone to travel with, or help when travelling), whether you travel solo or in a group, the group size, age ranges (for example 20–30, not exact ages), an amount you're willing to offer, a note, and optionally a WhatsApp number.

Messages and activity. Connection requests, the text messages you send in chats, ratings and reviews you give or receive, people you block, and reports you make.

Technical information. Like most online services, our servers record basic request information such as IP address, date and time, and the request made. We use this to keep the service running, prevent abuse (for example spam or too many requests), and fix problems.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos from your device, or payment details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your flight to other travellers on the same flight and let them connect with you.
  • To deliver your messages and show ratings on profiles.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports, enforcing blocks, and preventing spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the app.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to this policy.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you for marketing.

3. What other users can see

  • Anyone using SameFlight can see your name, profile photo, bio, languages, when you joined, your trip and connection counts, your rating and reviews, and the flight details you add (including your note, age ranges, travel type and any amount you offer).
  • Travellers you accept a chat with can also see your WhatsApp number, if you added one, and the messages in your chat with them.
  • Nobody else can see your email address, phone number, password, or the people you've blocked or reported.

Please only share what you're comfortable with other travellers seeing. When you share contact details or meet someone, that information is in their hands too.

4. We don't sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing or content. We only share information in these limited cases:

  • Hosting and infrastructure. We use trusted service providers to host our database, handle sign-in, and run our servers. They process data only on our behalf, to provide the service, and are not allowed to use it for anything else.
  • Flight number lookup. If you use "Look up flight", we send only the flight number (for example QR 730) to a flight-data provider to fill in the route and time. No personal information is sent.
  • Legal reasons. If we're required to by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.
  • Business changes. If SameFlight is merged or acquired, your information may transfer as part of that, under the same protections described here. We'll let you know before that happens.

5. Payments and agreements between travellers

SameFlight only helps travellers find and talk to each other. We don't take, hold or process any payments, and we aren't part of any arrangement you make with another traveller. Any amount shown on a flight is an offer between travellers; how you agree, pay, or meet is entirely between you and them, at your own discretion and responsibility. Please take care when meeting people you don't know, and report anyone who behaves inappropriately.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account is open. Flights stay on your account until you remove them. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, flights, chats, messages, ratings, blocks and reports linked to you. Copies in our secure backups are removed as the backups expire, and server logs are kept only for a short period.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and flight details at any time in the app.
  • Remove a flight from your trips at any time.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile or your chat with them.
  • Delete your account and data in the app under Profile → Delete your data. This can't be undone.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us (see below).

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under data protection laws (such as the GDPR or CCPA). Contact us and we'll help.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, secure sign-in tokens, access controls on our database, and limits on requests to protect your information. No service can be perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe and will notify you if a breach affects you, as required by law.

9. Age requirement

SameFlight is meant for people aged 18 and over. Children may only be included as members of a group listed by an adult, and only as an age range. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we'll remove it.

10. International use

Our servers and service providers may be located in a different country from you. By using SameFlight, your information may be processed there, with the protections described in this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll let you know in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email us at sameflightapp@gmail.com.
