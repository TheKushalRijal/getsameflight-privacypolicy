Delete your account and data

For the SameFlight app, by Kushal Rijal

You can delete your SameFlight account and all the data linked to it at any time. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us by email.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open SameFlight and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete your data and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't sign in or no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Or write to sameflightapp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my SameFlight account". We may reply to confirm the account is yours before deleting it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address and password.
  • Your profile: name, photo, bio, languages and phone number.
  • Your flights and their details: notes, age ranges, amounts and WhatsApp numbers.
  • Your chats and the messages in them.
  • Ratings and reviews you gave or received.
  • People you blocked and reports you made.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Backups: copies in our secure backups are removed automatically as the backups expire, within 30 days.
  • Server logs: basic request logs (such as IP address and time) are kept for up to 30 days for security, then deleted.
  • Legal reasons: we only keep something longer if the law requires it.

Delete some of your data, keep your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data:

  • Remove a flight with the Delete button under "Your flights" on Home, or from Trips. This deletes that flight's note, age ranges, amount and WhatsApp number.
  • Edit your profile to clear your bio, languages or photo.
  • Email us at sameflightapp@gmail.com to ask us to delete anything else, such as a specific chat. We'll do it within 30 days.
